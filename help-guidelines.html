Help & Guidelines

Find answers to common questions.

How to report an issue?

Follow these steps to report an issue:

  • Open the Report an Issue page.
  • Select the appropriate issue category.
  • Enter a clear description of your problem.
  • Upload a screenshot if necessary.
  • Click the Submit Issue button.

After submission, a unique Ticket ID will be generated for your issue.

What is a Ticket ID?

A Ticket ID is a unique identification number assigned to every reported issue.

For example: EMC-1001

You can use this ID to search for your issue and check its current status.

How to check status?

You can check your issue status by following these steps:

  • Open the Track Ticket page.
  • Enter your Ticket ID.
  • Click the Search button.
  • Your current issue status will be displayed.

Your issue may have one of these statuses:

  • Pending
  • In Progress
  • Resolved
Common Issues

Some common issues that can be reported through the EMC Issue Tracker include:

  • Unable to log in to the EMC portal.
  • Incorrect project status.
  • Profile information not updating.
  • Technical errors on the website.
  • Other portal-related problems.

Choose the correct category while reporting your issue.

Contact Support

Still facing a problem?

Please contact the appropriate EMC support team or your college administrator for further assistance.

Make sure to provide your Ticket ID and a clear explanation of your issue.
